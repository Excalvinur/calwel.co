Hi! I'm Connor, a designer and animator passionate about creating compelling visual experiences. With a background in motion design and digital creativity, I work with brands and businesses to bring their ideas to life through animation, web design, and creative direction.

When I'm not working on client projects, you'll find me exploring new design trends, experimenting with animation techniques, or collaborating with other creative professionals. I believe in the power of thoughtful design to communicate ideas and connect with audiences.

I'm always interested in taking on new projects and meeting creative individuals. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to collaborate or just chat about design!
